## ━━ SECTION 8: FAQ ━━

**Section label:** `FAQ`

**H2 Headline:**
```
Frequently Asked Questions
```

***

**Q: How accurate are the AI appraisals?**
A: Our AI delivers strong preliminary valuations based on visual analysis and cross-referenced market data. For high-value items (above $1,000), we recommend using our report as a starting document and following up with a certified appraiser for formal confirmation.

***

**Q: How many photos should I upload?**
A: 2–3 photos give the best results: one full view, one close-up of any markings or signatures, and one showing overall condition. Multiple angles significantly improve accuracy.

***

**Q: What types of antiques does the platform support?**
A: Furniture, ceramics, silverware, jewelry, paintings, clocks, textiles, militaria, and more — across a wide range of periods and cultural origins, including non-Latin inscriptions and Asian markings.

***

**Q: Do my tokens expire?**
A: No. Token credits never expire and can be used at any time.

***

**Q: Is this suitable for insurance or estate planning?**
A: Our Full Appraisal Reports follow IVS-aligned methodology and serve as a strong reference document. For formal insurance or legal estate purposes, your insurer or jurisdiction may additionally require a certified appraiser's signature.

***

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. Your uploaded images and appraisal data are stored securely with row-level security. We do not share or sell your data.
